Photo size guide

Minimum 1600×900px (16:9), landscape orientation. JPG, under ~500KB. These photos fill the entire screen behind the scrolling story and get cropped to fit — center the important part of the shot, since edges may be trimmed differently on phones vs. desktop. Avoid photos with important detail near the very top or bottom edge.

Turn any of these off to hide that whole section or card from the live site — no code changes needed. The hero story and Contact section aren't included here since hiding either would break the page.
